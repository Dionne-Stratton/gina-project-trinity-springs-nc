# Trinity Springs Christian Academy

Website project for Trinity Springs Christian Academy (TSCA), a Christ-centered classical homeschool hybrid program serving families in Sanford, North Carolina.

## About the Project

This project is the design and development of a new public-facing website for Trinity Springs Christian Academy. The site is intended to introduce prospective families to TSCA, explain its homeschool partnership model and classical Christian approach, provide admissions and tuition information, and make important resources easier to access.

The website is being developed as a custom React application rather than through a website builder, allowing the project to grow as TSCA's needs evolve.

## About Trinity Springs

Trinity Springs Christian Academy was established in 2023 with the mission:

> To bring glory to God through helping homeschool families develop the faith, character, and intellect of their children through Christ-centered classical instruction.

Its vision is:

> To make homeschooling accessible to families in our community.

TSCA operates as a homeschool hybrid program in which parents remain the primary educators of their children while partnering with instructors for classroom instruction on community days and structured learning at home.

## Planned Website Content

The site will include information about:

- Trinity Springs and its mission, vision, and statement of faith
- The homeschool hybrid model
- Classical Christian education
- Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric stages
- Admissions and enrollment
- Tuition and fees
- Student and community life
- Resources for current families
- Contact information and campus tours

Additional functionality may be added as project requirements are finalized.
