Statements: Mission, Vision, Faith 
Mission Statement:  
To bring glory to God through helping homeschool families develop the faith, character, and 
intellect of their children through Christ-centered classical instruction. 
Vision Statement:  
To make homeschooling accessible to families in our community. 
Statement of Faith: 
We are a Non-denominational Community. Our Statement of Faith reflects only the 
fundamental beliefs of Christianity because we are not a church and we are ecumenical. For 
theology beyond what is reflected here, we direct our students to the authority of their 
parents and their churches. We are, however, unapologetic in our belief in a loving, moral, 
and just God and His place in history and our lives. We desire to introduce our students to 
the Creator and the Savior, but the relationship must be theirs. 
•
We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God. 
•
We believe there is only one God, eternally existent in three Persons, Father, Son, and 
Holy Spirit. 
•
We believe Jesus was fully God and fully man. He died and rose physically for the 
redemption of all who will believe. 
•
We believe Jesus is the only Way and Provision for redemption. 
•
We believe God, the Creator, is a loving and compassionate God who hears prayer, 
speaks through His Holy Spirit, and intervenes for His people throughout history 
and still today. 
•
We believe the God of the Bible created the Earth and that humans, who are body, 
soul, and spirit, are made in His image. 
•
We believe that our sexual identity as male or female is inscribed in us by God and 
the marital union of male and female is a fundamental dimension of being made in 
the image of God. 
•
We believe in the spiritual unity of all believers in our Lord Jesus Christ. 
•
Eternal life is a free gift that is received through faith in Jesus alone. Eternal life is 
neither deserved by anyone, nor can it be earned by good deeds.

Our Partnership Model 
Parents are a child’s lifetime teachers and are commissioned by God to train them mind, 
body, and spirit. Academic pursuits are incredibly valuable, but a child's everyday life 
experiences are some of their richest educational opportunities. 
Trinity Springs provides classroom instruction on community days, and Trinity Springs 
parents commit time and effort as facilitators and instructors on at-home days. It is the 
parents role to encourage, supervise, and motivate their students through their educational 
process. In order for a hybrid model partnership to be most effective, parents and 
instructors must work together and communicate effectively about the students’ work and 
progress. It is our hope and our goal that our program instructors and our parents can 
partner together to provide high quality academics and foster the love of learning for each 
student.  
Hybrid Homeschool Program  
It is important to understand that Trinity Springs Christian Academy is not a school, but a 
homeschool hybrid program. Each family operates their own homeschool under the laws 
established by the NC Department of Non-Public Education, and is solely responsible for 
the maintenance of their homeschool. Each family is the ultimate authority in their 
children’s education. Therefore, homeschooling parents/guardians, are responsible for the 
schooling of their children, and it is their sole responsibility to ensure that all of their 
educational needs are met. Each family must maintain their own official records. Though 
TSCA cannot do this for our families, we may be able to assist, or direct a family to 
assistance, if that is needed.  
Mutual Respect for Traditions of our Faith 
Trinity Springs is a non-denominational homeschool program and therefore respects the 
right of families to practice their Christian faith in the denomination of their choice. 
Naturally, there will be points of doctrine and tradition which differ from one family to the 
next. While a point of doctrine may be introduced or discussed academically, Trinity 
Springs endeavors not to promote the acceptance of or the denial of any Church doctrine 
not set forth in the Statement of Fatih. Likewise, TSCA expects that all members of our 
community —instructors, volunteers, families, members, and other participants —practice 
understanding and respect for the various traditions of our Cristian faith.  
Conflict Resolution 
In the event of a conflict of any variety within our community, we humbly ask that those 
involved look to the example laid out in Matthew 18. We ask that individuals discreetly take 
grievances to the person(s) with whom there is a grievance first. If the issue cannot be 
resolved at the individual level, it may be elevated and brought to administration and/or 
the leadership team to help facilitate resolution.

ENROLLMENT PROCESS 
1. CAMPUS TOUR/FAMILY INTERVIEW 
We consider the tour and interview to be vital in the decision making process for prospective families. This 
is an opportunity for parents to see how their students interact with the people and environment of our 
community. The family interview is a natural but focused conversation, conducted during the tour, between 
a member of the administration, parents, and their children. The interview is an opportunity for you to get 
to know us and ensure all of your questions are answered. We want to be sure you feel confident that 
Trinity Springs Christian Academy is the best fit for your family. 
2. SUBMIT APPLICATION 
The next step is to submit the family application. Your application will be reviewed by our admissions 
committee. You may email applications to: info@TrinitySpringsNC.org 
3. ACCEPTANCE & ENROLLMENT 
Once your child is accepted for enrollment with TSCA, you will receive an enrollment packet, including all 
necessary forms and agreements, as well as important resources to ensure that your family has a successful 
start to the school year.

Trinity Springs Community Service Teams 
TSCA is an educational partnership between each family and our community. Serving within the 
community fosters a sense of belonging and camaraderie among the families and makes our 
programing possible. Our service teams also help keep our tuition and fees low. TSCA families are 
required to contribute a minimum of 15 hours of service within the TSCA community during the 10 
month academic year. Families experiencing exceptional circumstances may apply for an exception 
with administration.  
Service Team Opportunities 
Below is a list of some of the areas your family can serve. 
Campus Stewardship 
Facility Care Team - This team ensures that all areas used by our community are left in the condition we 
found them or better. This team will verify that our students completed their cleaning tasks as they should. 
These areas include the hallways, bathrooms, assembly room, and other common use areas. (i.e. make 
sure these areas are free of trash, spills, etc…) [Lunch Clean-up and classroom clean-up are not included 
in this team’s responsibilities]. 
Teacher Substitutes  –These parents will be available in the event one of our teachers is ill or otherwise 
unable to work. These parents may need to be available on relatively short notice. In the event of a 
teacher absence, TSCA will make every effort to provide lesson plans and materials for class. This year 
we have built substitutes into our staff, but we would be remiss to be unprepared for unforeseen events.  
Reading Helpers - These helpers come to campus to assist during periodic phonics assessments and to 
help our youngest readers get a little extra reading practice. This team could also read to our students 
during literature/story time. 
Community Building 
Photo Team – Take and/or collect photos throughout the year and possibly create a slideshow for the end 
of year program, take class photos, create a Memory Book/Yearbook for families to purchase. (Some 
faculty needed) 
Meal Train Organizer – Creates and manages “Meal Train” for families in our community in case of a 
life event. i.e.: new baby, surgery, illness, etc… 
Activities & Fundraising Planning Team - This team Coordinates & tracks the planning of community 
field trips, activities, special events, & fund raisers which are planned or hosted for our community. This 
team communicates with our families about upcoming field trips and activities and coordinates sign-ups 
and RSVPs.  
Special Events Team – This is a sub-team under the Activities & Fundraising team. Members of this 
team will help complete tasks and run event activities the day of the event. The Special Events team helps 
set up, run, and break down special events. These volunteers will communicate and coordinate with the 
Activities and Fundraising team about upcoming events. Some events might include class parties, end of 
semester programs, field day, etc…This is the perfect team for the volunteer who loves to help, but 
doesn’t love to plan.  
Special Lunch Team - This team comes to campus to help set up, serve, and clean up our monthly 
special lunches.

Uniform Policy 
The Trinity Springs Christian Academy uniform policy is intended to keep the focus on learning and 
away from clothing distractions. Our uniform choices have been made with modesty, comfort, and 
budget in mind. We expect students to arrive for classes each community day wearing clean and 
intact uniform attire. We do have occasional dress-down days on which students may express their 
sense of style with their clothing. 
Girls  
Upper Body: 
• Students may wear a collared, polo-style shirt. Classic & Peter Pan collars are acceptable.  
• Accepted polo shirt colors include navy, light blue, gray, and yellow. 
• Oxford shirts and Peter Pan collar blouses in white or light blue are acceptable to wear if preferred.  
• Sweaters are permitted in any style, with an accepted color of navy blue or heather gray.  
• Solid navy blazers are optional for upper grade. 
Lower Body: 
• Lands End Classic Navy plaid or French Toast Blue Gold Plaid skirt/skort or a solid navy or gray skirt/skort may 
be worn. Skirts/skorts should be no higher than the top of the knee. 
• White, navy, gray, or black knee high or crew socks should be worn with skirts/skorts. Tights in these colors are 
also acceptable. 
• Lower grade girls may wear a Classic Navy plaid jumper or solid navy polo dress if preferred. 
• Chino or jogger style pants may be worn with accepted colors of navy or gray. 
• Shoes: Students are permitted to wear neutral colored shoes. Either sneakers (tennis shoes) or Mary Jane style 
dress shoes are allowed. NO flip flops, open toe, or open back shoes 
Boys 
Upper Body: 
• Students may wear a collared, polo-style shirt.  
• Accepted polo shirt colors include navy, light blue, gray, and yellow.  
• Oxford shirts in white or light blue are acceptable to wear with an optional Classic Navy Plaid tie. 
• Sweaters are permitted in any style, with an accepted color of heather gray or navy. 
• Solid navy blazers are optional for upper grade. 
Lower Body: 
• Chino or jogger style pants may be worn with accepted colors of navy or gray. 
• Shorts may be worn with accepted colors of navy or gray. Shorts may be no more than 3 inches above the knee 
• White, navy, gray, or black crew socks are acceptable. 
• Shoes: Students are permitted to wear neutral colored shoes. Either sneakers (tennis shoes) or dress shoes are 
allowed. NO flip flops, open toe, or open back shoes
