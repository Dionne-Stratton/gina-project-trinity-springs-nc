# Trinity Springs Christian Academy Website Brief

## Project Goal

Build a welcoming, professional website for Trinity Springs Christian Academy (TSCA), a Christ-centered classical homeschool hybrid program in Sanford, North Carolina.

The primary audience is prospective homeschool families. The website should quickly communicate what Trinity Springs is, how its hybrid model works, what makes its approach distinctive, and how interested families can take the next step.

The site should also provide useful information and resources for current families without allowing policy-heavy material to overwhelm the public-facing experience.

## Primary Source Material

The `resources` folder contains official Trinity Springs documents supplied by the organization.

Use these documents as the authoritative source for TSCA-specific information, including:

- Mission and vision
- Statement of faith
- Homeschool hybrid model
- Classical instruction
- Admissions and enrollment
- Tuition and fees
- Calendar
- Leadership structure
- Community expectations
- Uniforms and policies
- Contact information

Do not invent missing organizational information. Flag conflicting or unclear information for confirmation.

## Core Messaging

The website should communicate that Trinity Springs:

- Is Christ-centered
- Uses classical instruction
- Partners with homeschool parents
- Provides classroom instruction on community days
- Supports structured learning at home
- Serves students through the Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric stages
- Values faith, character, intellect, family, and community
- Exists to help make homeschooling accessible to local families

TSCA describes itself as a homeschool hybrid program rather than a conventional school. Families maintain responsibility for their individual homeschools under North Carolina homeschool requirements.

## Homepage Direction

The homepage should function primarily as an introduction for prospective families.

Likely content flow:

1. Strong visual hero with concise identity statement and primary call to action
2. Introduction to Trinity Springs
3. Explanation of the homeschool hybrid model
4. Why classical Christian education
5. Grammar, Logic, and Rhetoric overview
6. Mission / faith / community
7. Admissions or tuition introduction
8. Invitation to schedule a tour, request information, or apply
9. Footer with essential organization information

Avoid turning the homepage into a reproduction of the information packet.

## Visual Direction

The site should feel:

- Warm
- Welcoming
- Christ-centered
- Family-oriented
- Established and trustworthy
- Academically serious without feeling institutional
- Classical without looking antiquated

Actual Trinity Springs photography should be used prominently once approved images are available.

Official logo and branding assets should take precedence over assumptions derived from reference websites.

## Functionality

Initial development should focus on the public-facing informational website.

Likely functionality includes:

- Responsive navigation
- Multi-page routing
- Contact/inquiry form
- Schedule-a-tour call to action
- Admissions/application call to action
- Links or downloads for family resources

Final handling of applications, enrollment, tuition payments, and other transactional features has not yet been determined.

Do not build a custom payment, enrollment, authentication, or backend system unless requirements are explicitly added later.

## Development Approach

Build incrementally.

Do not make large unsolicited architectural, dependency, content, or design changes. Prefer small, reviewable stages so each part of the site can be evaluated before moving to the next.
